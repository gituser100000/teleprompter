Running in Safari — not fullscreen

Safari keeps its address bar and tabs on screen, which eats a lot of a landscape phone. Installing the app to your Home Screen removes them completely.

In Safari: tap the Share button (the square with the up arrow) → Add to Home Screen → Add.
Then close Safari and launch the app from the new icon.

Rig — mirror & rotation

Controls & setup notes

While scrolling: tap the screen to pause/resume. Drag up or down to scrub to any point. The control bar hides itself after a few seconds — tap once to bring it back.

Bluetooth pedal / keyboard: Space play/pause · ↑↓ speed · ←→ jump a screen · B back a few lines · [] margins · R restart · M mirror · Esc exit. Most cheap page-turner pedals send arrow keys, so they work here with no pairing setup beyond Bluetooth itself.

Beam-splitter glass: the glass reverses the image, so turn on one of the mirror flips. Which one depends on how the phone sits in the cradle — try Mirror ↔ horizontal first, and if the text reads backwards in the glass, switch to the vertical one instead.

Rotate lets you keep iOS rotation lock ON and still get landscape text — handy once the phone is clamped in the rig and you don't want iOS deciding to flip on you mid-take.

Everything saves automatically on this device. No account, no internet needed after first load.
